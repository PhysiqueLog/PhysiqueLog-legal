Privacy Policy

PhysiqueLog · Last updated: 7 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PhysiqueLog ("we", "us", "the app") collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use the PhysiqueLog mobile application. By using the app, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: When you create an account, we collect your email address and a securely hashed password.
  • Progress photos: Photos you choose to capture in the app are stored to provide the core functionality of tracking your physique over time. These photos are personal and may be considered sensitive. They are only ever associated with your account.
  • App preferences: Settings such as daily reminder times, stored to operate the app.

We do not collect your name, location, contacts, advertising identifiers, or analytics/tracking data. We do not use third-party advertising.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate you and keep your account secure.
  • To store and display your progress photos back to you.
  • To send the daily reminder notifications you opt into.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information or photos with third parties for marketing.

3. Where Your Data Is Stored

Your account data and photos are stored using Supabase, our backend and storage provider, which hosts data on secure cloud infrastructure. Access to your photos is restricted to your authenticated account through access-control policies. Supabase processes data on our behalf and is contractually required to protect it. See Supabase's privacy practices at https://supabase.com/privacy.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Your photos are retained until you delete them or delete your account.
  • You can delete any individual photo at any time within the app.
  • To delete your entire account and all associated data, contact us at the email below and we will permanently delete your data within 30 days.

5. Security

We use industry-standard measures including encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and access controls to protect your data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to safeguard your information.

6. Children's Privacy

PhysiqueLog is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will remove it.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us at the email below.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
